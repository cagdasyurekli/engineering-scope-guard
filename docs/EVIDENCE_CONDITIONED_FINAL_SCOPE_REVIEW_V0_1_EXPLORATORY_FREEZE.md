## Derivation

The pinned dataset files, source revision, frozen design bytes, treatment
bytes, selection seed, schedule seed, and starting post-Pilot-v3 opaque reserve
commitment all matched their authorities. Metadata-only reconstruction yielded
462 tasks across 199 repositories before selection. Historical development,
Pilot, successor/continuation, canary, and host-replacement exposure remained
outside that reserve by task and repository.

Selection used only the frozen operational metadata projection and exact
language-by-language SHA-256 rank. No task body, solution, patch, difficulty,
expected success, semantic similarity, prior outcome, or evaluator result was
used. Each selected official container manifest was available and digest-bound
without pulling, running, or evaluating its image.

Repository-level removal of all selected-repository tasks leaves an opaque
confirmatory reserve of 434 tasks across 191 repositories. Its remaining
identities are not published; the machine artifact records only its count,
repository count, source provenance, domain, and commitment. Deterministic
regeneration proves the two partitions are repository-disjoint.

## Schedule and authority

The materialized schedule contains exactly 16 task-repetition blocks and 32
cells. Each two-arm block is contiguous. Every task is baseline-first once and
treatment-first once, and the block order is bound to the frozen pool
commitment and schedule seed. Manual, fallback, adaptive, and outcome-dependent
replacement or reordering are unauthorized.

This freeze created zero subject/provider calls, zero experimental evaluator
calls, zero experimental observations, and no execution ledger. It does not
establish efficacy, power, universal lack of bias, or execution readiness.
Experimental execution and any next goal require fresh explicit authorization.
