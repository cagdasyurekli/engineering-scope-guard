# Publication and Editorial Policy

## Evidence integrity

Public research claims must remain traceable to versioned evidence, distinguish
exploratory from confirmatory work, preserve adverse and null results, and state
material limitations and corrections.

## Independence and incentives

Financial, professional, commercial, sponsorship, or platform incentives must
not alter research conclusions, evidence classification, publication decisions,
or corrections.

## External presentation boundary

External publication and distribution channels are outside the scientific
record and require separate project decisions. External presentation must not
strengthen claims beyond the repository evidence.

## Corrections

Material errors must be corrected visibly without silently rewriting scientific
results or claim history.
